> An intelligent, open-source quiz platform designed for students at Vietnam National University of Science (VNU-HUS).  
> **VNU AI Quiz** leverages artificial intelligence to create an engaging, dynamic, and personalized learning experience.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://vnuaiquiz-roly.vercel.app/](https://vnuaiquiz-roly.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

* **🤖 AI-Powered Question Generation:** Automatically generate relevant and challenging questions tailored to VNU curriculums.
* **🎨 Minimalist UI/UX:** A clean, user-friendly interface designed for focus and ease of use.
* **🌓 Dark/Light Mode:** Seamlessly switch between themes for comfortable studying at any time of day.
* **⚡ Fast & Responsive:** Built for speed, ensuring a smooth experience across all devices.
* **📊 Performance Tracking:** (Planned) Track your progress and identify areas for improvement.
